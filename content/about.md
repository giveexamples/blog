GiveExamples.com is the personal technical blog of **Saeed Afshari**.

Hi, I'm **Saeed Afshari**, a Staff Software Engineer, software architect, and engineering consultant based in Munich, Germany.

I have more than **15 years of experience designing, building, and operating production software systems**, ranging from financial and telecommunications platforms to cloud-native distributed systems and enterprise security infrastructure.

My work sits at the intersection of **software architecture, distributed systems, platform engineering, reliability, and security**. I enjoy working on problems where there is no obvious answer: systems that have become difficult to scale, architectures that need to evolve, reliability issues that cross team boundaries, or technical decisions that will influence an organization for years.

I created **GiveExamples.com** to write about those problems using practical examples rather than abstract theory.

## Engineering Consulting

Alongside my primary role, I provide a limited amount of independent consulting to engineering organizations.

I primarily help **CTOs, engineering leaders, and software teams** with difficult architecture, platform, reliability, and security problems.

Typical engagements include:

### Architecture Reviews

Independent assessment of an existing software architecture to identify scalability constraints, unnecessary complexity, coupling, reliability risks, and areas where technical debt is slowing down engineering teams.

The objective is not simply to produce an architecture diagram. It is to identify the highest-leverage improvements and establish a practical path forward.

### Distributed Systems & Platform Architecture

Advice on service boundaries, event-driven systems, Kafka, data ownership, cloud-native architecture, Kubernetes, platform engineering, and infrastructure design.

I can help teams evaluate architectural choices before committing to expensive migrations or large-scale redesigns.

### Reliability & Production Engineering

Assessment of production reliability, observability, operational ownership, SLIs/SLOs, error budgets, failure modes, and engineering practices around running distributed systems in production.

### Identity & Security Architecture

Architecture and governance reviews covering authentication, authorization, OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, Microsoft Entra ID, application identities, service principals, Infrastructure as Code, and enterprise identity governance.

### Technical Strategy

Support for engineering organizations facing architectural decisions that span multiple teams or systems.

This can include architecture modernization, technical roadmaps, ADRs, platform strategy, migration approaches, and evaluating trade-offs between competing solutions.

I generally work best on **short, focused engagements where an experienced independent technical perspective can unblock an important decision or uncover risks early**.

If your engineering organization is facing a difficult architecture, reliability, platform, or security problem, feel free to [contact me](mailto:saeed.afshari8@gmail.com) for an initial conversation.

## Why GiveExamples?

A lot of technical material explains *what* a technology does.

I am more interested in:

**When should we use it?**

**What happens when it reaches production?**

**What are the trade-offs?**

**What breaks at scale?**

**How does the decision affect other teams?**

**What does the implementation actually look like?**

That is the idea behind the name **GiveExamples**.

I use this blog to document engineering concepts, architecture patterns, experiments, lessons learned, and practical implementations around topics such as:

* Software architecture
* Distributed systems
* Cloud engineering
* Kubernetes
* Infrastructure as Code
* Identity and access management
* Application security
* Reliability engineering
* AI-assisted software engineering
* Practical engineering patterns

The opinions expressed here are my own. Examples are created for educational purposes and do not disclose confidential information from employers or clients.
